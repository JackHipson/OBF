# MBR Commentary Generator

Help Jack draft Monthly Business Review commentary for Oakbrook.

## Key Metrics Context

| Metric | Full Name | What It Means |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| NBV | Net Book Value | Loan book size - total outstanding loans |
| Yield | Interest Yield | Interest income as % of loan book |
| Impairment | Loan Impairment | Bad debt provisions and write-offs |
| CoF | Cost of Funds | Interest paid on borrowings |
| PBT | Profit Before Tax | Bottom line before tax |
| LEC | Loan Equivalent Cost | Cost per loan originated |
| APR | Annual Percentage Rate | Customer-facing interest rate |
| IRR | Internal Rate of Return | Return on investment measure |

## Commentary Structure

### 1. Executive Summary (2-3 sentences)
- Overall performance vs budget/prior period
- Key driver of variance
- Outlook statement

### 2. Revenue Analysis
- NBV movement (growth/contraction)
- Yield performance
- New business vs back-book contribution

### 3. Cost Analysis
- Impairment trends
- Cost of funds movement
- Operating cost variances

### 4. Key Callouts
- Significant one-offs
- Risks or concerns
- Opportunities identified

## Writing Guidelines

- **Be specific**: "NBV grew 5% to £X" not "NBV increased"
- **Explain variances**: Why, not just what
- **Forward-looking**: Include outlook where relevant
- **Audience-aware**: Board = strategic; Will = operational detail
- **Keep it tight**: One paragraph per section max
