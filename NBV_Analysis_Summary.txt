NBV DISCREPANCY ANALYSIS
========================

OBSERVATION: "NBV is below budget but balance sheet disbursals are above budget"

ROOT CAUSE:
-----------
Higher actual collection rates than budgeted.

KEY METRICS:
- Actual collection rate (6-month avg): ~-7.35%
- Budget collection rate: ~-5.4% to -6.1%
- V3.2 collection rate: ~-6.7%

EXPLANATION:
The budget assumed collection rates of approximately -5.4% to -6.1%.
Actual collection rates have been running at approximately -7.35%.

This means the loan portfolio is being paid down FASTER than expected.
Higher collections = Lower GBV = Lower NBV.

WHY DISBURSALS DON'T HELP:
Even though disbursals are above budget (which adds to GBV),
the collections are ALSO above budget by an even greater margin.

Example Impact:
- £276M starting GBV
- 1.5pp higher collection rate = £4.1M more collections per month
- Over 6 months = ~£25M more collections than budget
- Net result: Lower NBV despite higher disbursals

CONCLUSION:
The original budget collection rate assumptions were too conservative.
The v3.2 model (using ~-6.7% collection rate) is closer to reality
but still slightly optimistic compared to actual -7.35%.
