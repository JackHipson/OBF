NBV DISCREPANCY ANALYSIS - REVISED FINDINGS

CREDIT TEAM OBSERVATION:
"NBV is below budget but balance sheet disbursals are above budget"

================================================================================
KEY FINDING: CONTRA SETTLEMENTS ARE NOT MODELED
================================================================================

Both the original budget (PQ) and the v3.2 Python model assume ZERO contra 
settlements. However, actuals show ~1.2% of GBV per month is being removed 
through contra settlements from top-ups.

WHAT ARE CONTRA SETTLEMENTS?
- When a customer does a top-up, their old loan is "contra-settled" (closed)
- A new loan is created for the combined amount (old balance + top-up)
- The old loan's balance is removed from GBV via contra settlement

================================================================================
QUANTIFIED IMPACT
================================================================================

                                Budget/Model    Actuals     Gap
                                ------------    -------     ---
Regular Collection Rate:        -6.1%           -7.2%       -1.1pp
Contra Settlement Rate:          0.0%           -1.2%       -1.2pp
                                ------          ------      ------
TOTAL Outflow Rate:             -6.1%           -8.4%       -2.3pp

ANNUAL GBV IMPACT:
- Missing contra settlements:      ~£40M overstatement
- Higher regular collections:      ~£36M overstatement
- TOTAL GBV GAP:                   ~£50-60M over 12 months

================================================================================
WHY THIS EXPLAINS THE NBV SHORTFALL
================================================================================

1. Budget assumes GBV runs off at ~6.1% per month
2. Actual GBV runs off at ~8.4% per month (collections + contra settlements)
3. This 2.3pp difference compounds each month
4. Even with higher disbursals, the faster runoff means lower GBV
5. Lower GBV = Lower NBV (since NBV = GBV - Provision)

PREVIOUS ANALYSIS WAS INCOMPLETE:
We identified higher collection rates but missed that contra settlements
(which are SEPARATE from collections in the data) account for MORE than
half of the gap!
