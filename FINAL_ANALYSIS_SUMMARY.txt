================================================================================
KEY RATIOS DERIVABLE FROM THE DATA
================================================================================

1. Collections Rate = (Coll_Principal + Coll_Interest) / OpeningGBV
   - Measures cash collection efficiency
   - Typically 8-10% per month for this portfolio

2. Coverage Ratio = Provision Balance / GBV
   - Available in 11_Impairment sheet
   - Shows impairment provision as % of loan book

3. Impairment Rate = Net_Impairment / OpeningGBV
   - Monthly provision charge as % of portfolio
   - Can be volatile month-to-month

4. Interest Yield = InterestRevenue / OpeningGBV
   - Monthly interest yield
   - Typically 2-4% per month for subprime lending

5. GBV Runoff = (ClosingGBV / OpeningGBV) - 1
   - Portfolio decay rate
   - Should be consistent with collections + write-offs
